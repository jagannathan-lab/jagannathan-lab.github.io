We are excited to welcome <a href="/docs/spear_cv.pdf">Elizabeth Spear</a> to the lab as a new postdoctoral fellow. 

Elizabeth comes to us after earning a Ph. D. in Microbiology, working with the [Jeff Kieft](https://www.kieftlab.org/) and [Linda van Dyk](https://medschool.cuanschutz.edu/immunology-and-microbiology/about-us/faculty/primary-faculty/van-dyk) laboratories.
